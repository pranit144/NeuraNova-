## 🧠 What is this?

**AI Foundations** is the first phase of **AI Meme University** — a structured journey through Artificial Intelligence, starting from the absolute basics.

The goal isn't just to memorize definitions like:

> "AI is the simulation of human intelligence by machines."

That's boring. 💀

The goal is to understand:

* What AI actually is
* How AI evolved
* How machines learn
* How AI differs from ML and Deep Learning
* Why modern AI works
* Where today's AI systems fit
* What we'll eventually learn under the hood

# 💀 The Teaching Philosophy

This isn't meant to be another:

> **"AI in 10 minutes 🚀"**

tutorial.

The idea is:

### Understand → Break Down → Implement → Experiment → Meme

We will gradually move from:

```text
"What is AI?"
```

to:

```text
"How does a Transformer calculate attention?"
```

and eventually:

```text
"How do I build, evaluate,
deploy and optimize an AI system?"
```
