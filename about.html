🎯 What Is This?

AI Meme University is a comprehensive AI/ML learning platform built around a series of PDF presentations. Each lesson covers a specific topic, organized into phases that build on each other progressively.

The goal isn't just to memorize definitions like "AI is the simulation of human intelligence by machines." That's boring. 💀 The goal is to actually understand how things work under the hood.

🔧 How It Works

📖 PDF-Based Learning

Each lesson is a PDF presentation rendered directly in your browser using PDF.js. No downloads needed — just click and learn.

💾 Progress Tracking

Your progress is saved locally in your browser using localStorage. Mark lessons as complete, and the website remembers where you left off — even the exact PDF page.

🔍 Search

Use the global search to find any lesson by title, description, or phase. Need to find "transformer"? It'll show you every relevant lesson instantly.

📱 Responsive Design

Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile. The sidebar collapses into a hamburger menu on smaller screens, and the PDF viewer remains usable on all devices.

🤔 Philosophy

This platform follows a simple philosophy:

  • Sequential learning — topics build on each other
  • Depth over breadth — understand the "why", not just the "what"
  • Self-paced — go at your own speed
  • No fluff — every lesson has a purpose
  • Memes welcome — because learning should be fun 🧠
